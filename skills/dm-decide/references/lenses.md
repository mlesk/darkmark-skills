# Decision lenses

The lens menu `dm-decide` passes to `dm-grill`. Each lens must produce a question about *this* decision whose answer could move a score. Naming a lens without a finding is lens tourism.

## The seven core lenses

**1. First principles** — what is fundamentally true here, independent of how others do it?
- Probe: "Which assumptions are we inheriting without testing?"
- Example: "We assume we need funding before building. Do we, or do we need first customers?"

**2. Opportunity cost** — every option forecloses others.
- Probe: "If we choose A, what are we saying no to: money, time, focus, future options?"
- Example: "The conference is not $500. It is $500 plus the work you won't do that week."

**3. Second-order effects** — what happens next, and after that?
- Probe: "What is the first consequence, and what does it cause?"
- Example: "Skipping the event saves money now and removes next year's introductions."

**4. Compounding** — what do repeated versions of this choice build?
- Probe: "If we made this choice every week for two years, where would we end up?"
- Example: "One post is nothing. One a week for two years is an audience."

**5. Incentives** — who benefits from each option, including advisors and the decider?
- Probe: "What does each advisor gain if I pick their option? What do I want to believe?"
- Example: "The vendor recommends the top tier. Does that serve me or their quota?"

**6. Probabilistic thinking** — odds, not certainty.
- Probe: "How much does this option raise the probability of the outcome we want, at what cost? What is the worst realistic case?"
- Example: "The launch probably won't go viral, but it raises the odds of one enterprise lead."

**7. Inversion** — what would guarantee failure?
- Probe: "It is a year later and this failed. What happened?"
- Example: "We hired the same way in the new city and burned the runway in six months."

## Extra lenses (only if they move this decision)

- **Reversibility:** "If this is wrong, what does undoing it cost?"
- **Values:** "If you could keep only one option's outcome, which do you actually want?"
- **Total cost:** "What is the price beyond the sticker: switching, maintenance, attention?"
- **Timing:** "Is waiting cheaper than deciding now, or does the option expire?"

## How many lenses

| Decision | Lenses |
| --- | --- |
| Two-way door, low stakes | 2-3 that matter most, usually opportunity cost, inversion, and one more |
| Two-way door, high stakes | 4-5 |
| One-way door | All seven, skipping only those that cannot move a score (say why) |
