## Defects found in the skill

| # | Defect | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interactive sessions never took the session break | The first session reached 63 dispatches across human answers (the limit is about 25) | Check `session-dispatches` before every dispatch and break at 25, including in interactive sessions |
